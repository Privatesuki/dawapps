Tips

Web Serial and Web Bluetooth work in Chrome, Edge and Opera on desktop (and Chrome on Android for Bluetooth), over HTTPS or localhost. Safari and Firefox do not support them. Close the Arduino IDE serial monitor first — a port can only be opened by one program.
